# BATTLEGROUND X — MAP GUIDE

> 360×360 unit map | 8 named zones | 10 players | 7 safe-zone phases

---

## Map Overview

```
                    MILITARY BASE (North)
                          [ N ]

   PORT (NW)    SCHOOL (W)   |   POCHINKI (E)    SHELTER (NE)
        \           |        |        |           /
         -------- CENTER (roads cross) ---------
        /           |        |        |           \
   FARM (SW)    HOSPITAL(S)  |   RUINS (SE)
                          [ S ]
```

Roads connect all zones. Main cross road runs N-S and E-W through the center.

## Zone Details

### 🪖 MILITARY BASE — North (0, 80)
**Difficulty:** High | **Loot:** Best in map

| Building | Floors | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Command Center | 2 | Tallest building — sniper nest |
| Barracks A & B | 1 | Long corridors — close combat |
| Armory | 1 | Weapon/ammo spawns |
| Watchtower A & B | 1 (tall) | 360° view of north half |

**Strategy:**
- Land Watchtower for early AR spawn + map awareness
- Control Command Center = control the zone
- Perimeter walls create natural chokepoints — set crossfire here
- Runway connects to open field — don't cross it without cover

---

### 🏫 SCHOOL — West (-80, 0)
**Difficulty:** High | **Loot:** Good

| Building | Floors | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Main Building | 2 | Most rooms — highest loot density |
| Gymnasium | 1 | Large open floor — risky, high reward |
| Library | 2 | Tight stairs — perfect ambush |
| Canteen | 1 | Quick loot stop |

**Strategy:**
- Main Building 2nd floor window = best west-side snipe spot
- Gymnasium door = kill funnel — throw grenades first
- Fence perimeter gives cover approaching from outside

---

### 🏘️ POCHINKI — East (80, 0)
**Difficulty:** Medium | **Loot:** Good

| Building | Use |
|---|---|
| Church (center tall) | Tallest point — sniper + zone watching |
| Houses (8 total) | Scattered loot, close-quarters fights |

**Strategy:**
- Church steeple visible from entire east side — enemy will contest it
- Houses form a natural ring — rotate between them for cover
- Central position makes Pochinki a great mid-game rotation hub

---

### 🏥 HOSPITAL — South (0, -80)
**Difficulty:** Medium | **Loot:** Best medkit spawns

| Building | Notes |
|---|---|
| Main Wing + East/West Wing | H-shaped layout — complex rotation |
| Emergency | Ground floor only — fast loot |
| Helipad (rooftop) | Open — risky landing but fast rotation |

**Strategy:**
- Red cross on roof = visible from far — enemies know you're inside
- H-shape means 3 entry points — always cover all 3 when defending
- Best place to stock medkits before final circle

---

### 🚢 PORT — NW (-80, 80)
**Difficulty:** Medium | **Loot:** Medium + Unique (containers)

| Structure | Notes |
|---|---|
| Warehouses A & B | Long buildings — shotgun zone |
| Coloured Containers (6) | Stack and climb for high ground |
| Crane | Tallest point in Port — extreme snipe range |

**Strategy:**
- Containers = best climbing cover on map
- Crane top = risky climb but unmatched overwatch
- Port is corner zone — only 2 entry roads, easy to hold late game

---

### 🏚️ RUINS — SE (80, -80)
**Difficulty:** Low | **Loot:** Low-Medium

| Structure | Notes |
|---|---|
| Ruin Walls (8) | Partial walls — crouch cover only |

**Strategy:**
- Low loot but zero traffic — safe early land for passive players
- Ruin walls provide crouch cover — useful for 1v1 close range
- Rotate to Pochinki/Hospital via road for better loot mid-game

---

### 🌾 FARM — SW (-80, -80)
**Difficulty:** Low | **Loot:** Low-Medium

| Building | Notes |
|---|---|
| Farmhouse | Small — fast clear |
| Barn | Largest building in zone |
| Silos A & B | Tall cylinders — climb for south overwatch |

**Strategy:**
- Crop rows = prone cover — sneak through them
- Barn + Silos = good defensive position if safe zone pulls south
- Corner zone — quiet early game, good for healing up

---

### 🛖 SHELTER — NE (80, 80)
**Difficulty:** Low | **Loot:** Low

| Building | Notes |
|---|---|
| Bunker | Low ceiling — close quarters |
| Huts A & B | Small, quick loot |
| Watchtower | NE corner overwatch |

**Strategy:**
- Watchtower overlooks Military Base and Pochinki simultaneously
- Bunker = defensible final position if circle pulls NE

## Rotation Tips

1. **Early game** — land corner zones (Farm/Ruins/Shelter) for safe loot
2. **Mid game** — rotate via roads to School/Pochinki/Hospital
3. **Late game** — control Military Base or Port (corner = fewer attack angles)
4. **Final circle** — Hospital or Command Center rooftop = most wins recorded
